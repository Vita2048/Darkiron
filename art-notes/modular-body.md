# Darkiron modular body experiment

Use rigid, separate raster pieces driven by the existing vector skeleton. Keep the current head and rifle assets. Only the hero uses the experiment. Preserve collision, planted-foot gait, recoil, ladder controls and enemy rendering.

Joint strategy: rounded shoulder/elbow and hip/knee connector overlaps; near limbs above far limbs; torso above proximal thighs; small dark flexible abdomen beneath chest plates. Keep the existing animated trigger finger over the raster glove. Draw a separate glove at free/climbing wrists.

Fallback: player.hybrid_body can disable the new body parts at runtime without replacing the rig or gameplay code.
